06 — FAQ

Questions.

What is BACKR?

A way to launch a coin on any Hyperliquid vault. The coin's creator fees are deposited into the vault, so the coin owns a piece of the trader's book.

What is a Hyperliquid vault?

A pool run by a trader. Depositors share the vault's profits and losses, and the leader keeps a share of the profits. Everything about it, from PnL to depositors, is public on Hyperliquid.

Which vaults can a coin back?

Any open vault that takes deposits. The board lists them live, biggest first, with their real PnL.

How do I know a coin really backs its vault?

Paste the coin's treasury address into Check a backer. It reads the address's vault stakes straight from Hyperliquid.

Can a vault lose money?

Yes. A vault's PnL goes down as well as up, and the coin's stake moves with it. The board shows the real numbers, red included.

When can I launch my own?

Launches open after $BACKR goes live. Pick a vault and post your card now.

Is BACKR part of pump.fun or Hyperliquid?

No. It's independent and built on both. Vault leaders aren't affiliated with BACKR and don't endorse coins launched on their vaults.

Is this financial advice?

No. Past PnL doesn't predict future PnL, and nothing here is a promise of returns.
